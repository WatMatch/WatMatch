# Stakeholder Meeting - 2026-06-04

## Parties Involved

- Prof. Derek Rayside - Global Futures Initiative lead, WatMatch mentor.
- Mr. Jamieson Cox - Centre for Work-Integrated Learning, capstone/interdisciplinary workflow stakeholder.
- Siddharth / WatMatch team - SE 490 capstone team.

Meeting was recorded on MS Teams so future teammates can reference the discussion.

## Meeting Purpose

Review the current WatMatch direction after Pitch and Mid 1 implementation work, especially:

- Whether the proposed multi-course approval workflow matches how Waterloo capstones actually work.
- How students should explore interdisciplinary projects before committing.
- What roles and administrative workflows are missing.
- What details need to change in the student, instructor, and external partner flows.

## Key Stakeholder Feedback

WatMatch should feel more like a marketplace than a rigid one-to-one matching system.

Students should be able to explore multiple opportunities before converging to one official capstone/team.

The system should encourage non-engineering students and students without strict degree capstone requirements to participate in the capstone ecosystem.

The outgoing/home-course instructor should not block a student who wants to move into another capstone course.

Example:

- An `SE 490` student wants to join an `MTE 481` or `ECE 498A` capstone.
- The student's SE instructor should not need to approve or reject that move.
- The receiving course/instructor and advisor/enrollment process should handle whether the route is valid.

WatMatch should track students who want to transition between capstone courses.

The app needs more role separation. Not every admin-like user should see or control everything.

Potential roles discussed:

- Academic advisor.
- Enrollment operator/coordinator.
- Admin.
- Instructor.
- Mentor.
- External partner.

External partner workflows should more closely match the current Airtable form used by Waterloo.

Student profiles are valuable but underdeveloped. They should better support team formation and student discovery.
