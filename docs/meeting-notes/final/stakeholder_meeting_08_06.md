## Agenda

- Review the features completed for Final.
- Confirm the Fall 2026 pilot scope and rollout constraints.
- Prioritize the next features for the Fall WatMatch team.
- Clarify the University Relationship Manager workflow for FCI projects.

## What I Presented

- The completed Exploration, Commitment, Finalization, term-closeout, and Past Capstones lifecycle.
- Multi-course routing, role-specific workspaces, audit history, and manual Quest handoff.
- The proposed SE 490 Fall 2026 pilot and future-feature roadmap.

## Feedback

- An SE-only Fall 2026 pilot is a good starting point.
- Derek considered the limited pilot a useful option even if ADFS authentication is not provided.
- Derek asked the Fall team to decide how broadly WatMatch should be rolled out to students.
- Jamieson agreed that, if a pilot is needed, Software Engineering is an appropriate audience.
- ADFS could technically support a broader Waterloo rollout, but Jamieson does not have capacity to support a university-wide deployment. The practical Fall scope is SE.
- Using AWS compute credits alongside ADFS should be investigated, but Waterloo authentication access is still required.
- Multi-role accounts and role switching are necessary and should be the first Fall implementation priority.
- Instructor control of the three marketplace phases should be the second Fall implementation priority.
- Mentor, instructor, and TA milestone feedback is not currently a priority. The team should avoid trying to cover too much, especially because grades are sensitive information.
- Recording whether a team exists is less sensitive than storing grades or detailed academic evaluations.
- Email notifications are useful if they are selective and do not spam users.
- Project intake should consider non-commercial restrictions and provide a dropdown of applicable licence types alongside confidentiality and NDA requirements.
- FCI brings projects into i-Capstone and remains the primary holder of those external relationships.
- For an FCI-tagged project, a University Relationship Manager should be affiliated with the project, confirm that the match is viable, and participate in the kickoff with the student team and project mentor.
- The University Relationship Manager must be informed when a match occurs because the role has a concrete follow-up action.

## Decisions

- Scope the proposed Fall pilot to SE students.
- Prioritize multi-role accounts and role switching first.
- Prioritize instructor control of marketplace phases second.
- Keep grades outside the immediate WatMatch roadmap.
- Treat the University Relationship Manager as an actionable, project-specific role for FCI-affiliated projects.
