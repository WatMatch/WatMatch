## Agenda

- Align WatMatch's support roles before implementing the multi-course workflow.
- Clarify how interdisciplinary participation should be presented to students.
- Review how held-with courses affect each student's enrollment route.

## Notes

- The existing mentor concept needs to be separated into clearer responsibilities.
- A University Mentor is a Waterloo-based mentor who provides academic or technical project guidance.
- An External Partner is the organization or challenge owner working with the capstone team.
- The current Academic Advisor framing should become a Community Mentor role, similar to Angie's role in Software Engineering.
- A University Relationship Manager represents an institutional relationship, such as FCI's involvement with a project. Only a small number of projects require this role.
- The Enrollment Coordinator remains responsible for selecting each student's enrollment course and making the corresponding updates in Quest and WatMatch.
- Interdisciplinary opportunities should include a disclaimer that students must be open to working with people from many faculties and departments.
- Students on the same interdisciplinary project may need different transcript courses based on their home faculty or program.
- Fall 2026 example: `ARTS 450` is held with courses including `GENE 403`. An Arts student may be routed into `ARTS 450` while an Engineering teammate uses `GENE 403` or another approved Engineering route, even though they share the same project and course experience.

## Decision

Mid 3 will preserve one shared capstone/team while distinguishing project-support roles and recording a separate approved enrollment route for each student.
